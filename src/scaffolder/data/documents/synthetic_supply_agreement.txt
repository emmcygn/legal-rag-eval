SYNTHETIC COMPONENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

1 PAYMENT
1.1 Buyer must pay each undisputed invoice within twenty calendar days after receipt.

2 DELIVERY
2.1 Seller must dispatch components by the shipment date in the order form.
2.2 Buyer must report delayed delivery in writing within five business days after the shipment date.
2.3 A routine shipment notice does not constitute a delayed-delivery report.

3 REPLACEMENT
3.1 Seller will replace components that fail inspection before acceptance.
