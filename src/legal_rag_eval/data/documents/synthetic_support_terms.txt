SYNTHETIC SUPPORT TERMS

1 SUPPORT HOURS
1.1 Standard support operates from 09:00 to 17:00 on business days.

2 INCIDENTS
2.1 A critical incident means a complete interruption of the hosted service.
2.2 Routine maintenance announced two days in advance is not a critical incident.

3 CONTACT
3.1 Support requests must identify the account and describe the observed interruption.
